Growth Score Calculator

Growth Score: on a scale of 0 to 100, it evaluates the potential career growth a job offers using the average of 7 different metrics:

  • Salary
  • Culture and Diversity ratings
  • Career Opportunities provided
  • Job Openings
  • How likely you are to get promoted
  • Work-life balance score
  • Burnout rate (lower = better)
